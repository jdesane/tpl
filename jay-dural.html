06 · The Equity Story

Stock In The Brokerage
That Owns You.

Most agents will spend 20+ years building their brokerage's brand and walk away with nothing but a final commission check. LPT was built different. Production earns ownership.

How LPT Equity Works

Every year, LPT awards Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) based on your annual core transaction count. The awards stack: hit the Silver tier and you also get White. Hit Gold and you get White + Silver + Gold. Hit Black, you get all four. Each award vests over 3 years.

LPT Realty's parent company, Aperture Holdings, has reserved the ticker LPTA on Nasdaq. Liquidity depends on a future IPO, acquisition, or internal buyback. Not currently publicly traded.
